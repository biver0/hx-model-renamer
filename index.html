HX Effects · Model Renamer · NAM A2 Lite friendly

Rename the models on your HX Effects

Drop in your firmware and give every model a name that says what it is: Boss SD-1 instead of Stupor OD. Then download a new .hxf with every checksum fixed. It all runs in this browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Unofficial and experimental. This isn't made or supported by Line 6, and installing modified firmware is at your own risk. Only the model names change: no sound, preset or NAM code is touched. If anything goes wrong, the Recovery steps put the stock firmware back.

Install it

The same way as any Line 6 update.

  1. In HX Edit, back up your presets and IRs, then close HX Edit.
  2. If you got a .zip, open it to get the .hxf inside. Open Line 6 Updater, choose Choose File, and pick the renamed .hxf.
  3. Let it finish, and let the HX Effects restart on its own.
  4. Open the model list: Distortion → Mono should now show Boss SD-1 where Stupor OD was.

HX Edit still shows Line 6's names. It reads them from its own model list on your computer, not from the unit. Your presets are fine either way: they store each model's ID, not its name.

!

Recovery

If the unit doesn't start, or you want the original names back.

  1. Turn the HX Effects off. Hold the right arrow (>) while you turn it on: that's its fail-safe update mode.
  2. In Line 6 Updater, install the stock HX_Effects_v3.80.0_0xd97239907.hxf (or your NAM A2 Lite build).
What exactly does this change?

Two name fields per model in the unit's main application: the name in the model list (Table "browser", 26 bytes) and the model's own name (16 bytes). Then the application's image CRC, its archive CRC32 and Fletcher sums, the payload MD5 and the file size fields. The file's length doesn't change, and no other byte does.

Why is NAM A2 Lite locked?

That slot (Xenomorph Fuzz Mono in stock firmware) is where the NAM A2 Lite build keeps its block, and its name there came from that build. The stereo Xenomorph Fuzz is still the real fuzz, so you can rename that one.

What about the HX Stomp?

Not yet. The Stomp's firmware keeps these tables at other addresses, so it needs its own mapping. The page tells you if you drop a Stomp file.
